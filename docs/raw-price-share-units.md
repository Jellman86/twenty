A split alone cannot justify that gain. Freshness in days does not establish that a quote and quantity use the same share units. The pure-split paper seam already refuses that case; this checkpoint extends the protection to the shared reference and its consumers.

The shared rule is `price.SessionCloseAt >= latest known share-changing split.EffectiveAt` for the listing, otherwise `raw-price-share-units`. It applies to ordinary holdings, both settled/claim-valued target planners (including an unheld buy), pending successor stock and replay through these methods. Identity-ratio splits do not change share units; the paper guard now exempts them too. Unavailable future disclosures remain invisible through the point-in-time slice. No synthetic price conversion, rescaled past quote or adjusted-price fallback is invented. Inspection of old reports stays exact; their science is not rerun or rewritten silently.
