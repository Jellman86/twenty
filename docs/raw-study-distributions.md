# Registered per-share distributions

`raw-study-development-v3` extends the [registered raw worker](raw-study-development.md) beyond pure splits. The original action catalog now supports declared dividend payments and pure zero/cash/stock terminal outcomes across independent momentum, equal-weight and cash accounts. This is a bounded policy simulation. It does not import broker confirmations, establish tax treatment, authenticate source contents or certify achievable wealth.

## Frozen catalog contract

The catalog remains an original, hash-pinned JSON array. `studyDividend` and `studyTerminal` entries carry their original observation/cutoff proof, capture point and nullable delivery. Every supported economic action known and effective within the development window needs a matching original pin. Future final-window actions are excluded from development scheduling. The supplied file's coverage is checked; source-wide action discovery remains separate.

A dividend delivery names its booking point, source payment identity/reference/hash, actual and availability timestamps, currency, gross per share, withheld per share, fixed GBP fee, exact FX identifier and allocation rule. A terminal delivery additionally distinguishes cash consideration from successor shares per original share. The only supported allocation rule is `exact-decimal-per-cutoff-share-fixed-gbp-fee-v1`.

Gross per share and currency must match the corporate action. Withholding must be between zero and gross; fees cannot be negative. Stock delivery must match the original successor and exchange ratio and carry no cash, withholding or FX fields. Confirmed zero has no delivery. Missing proofs, unsupported rules, mixed consideration, duplicate points and wrong source bindings are refused. Constructors require explicit fields; zero withholding/fees are not inferred from omitted JSON. Admission validates these policies before committing any trial.

Amounts are derived from frozen legal-cutoff ownership: actual gross and withholding are each per-share amount multiplied by entitled quantity; successor delivery is the exchange ratio multiplied by retired quantity. The GBP fee is charged once per entitled account and is not multiplied by shares. An account without entitlement receives neither delivery nor fee. Exact decimal fractions are retained; broker rounding, cash in lieu, stock withholding, partial payments, custody fees and settlement-cycle funding restrictions are not silently approximated. These inputs are declared scenarios, not universal broker/tax rules.

Legacy offline `dividend`/`terminal` catalog entries retain their original absolute-amount meaning. They remain readable and supported by the standalone offline scheduler; registered execution refuses them with `raw-study-absolute-payment-unsupported`. A one-account statement amount is never implicitly spread across different strategy holdings.

## Scheduling and accounting

The existing source/vintage scheduler derives capture from the latest of effectiveness, action availability and cutoff-proof availability, and delivery from capture plus actual delivery/availability. Actual receipt amounts are allocated only after the ledger freezes entitlement. Payment FX remains pinned at actual payment/settlement, not the later booking or mark. Existing purpose, direction, freshness, denomination, source and revision guards remain active.

Ownership reconstruction includes modelled opening times, split effectiveness, terminal retirement and actual successor delivery. This permits a later dividend on delivered successor shares without inventing earlier ownership. Late disclosures after quantity changes still refuse historical restatement. Due accounting events precede a shared monthly mark/deposit; an outstanding ordered fill batch cannot be interrupted by another accounting operation.

Known deliveries after the development end are not brought forward. A pending dividend or terminal claim at any monthly mark refuses the trial through the ordinary ledger's unpaid/unsettled guard. Pending value is never dropped or used to fund the next rebalance. The separate explicit receivable-mark reference remains available offline; crossing a registered mark with a pending claim still needs a frozen valuation policy and planner integration. All failures remain registered and stop their remaining family.

Each receipt binds original plan/catalog bytes, source hashes, complete derived event order and ownership dispositions. Recovery reconstructs the request and independently replays ledger operations, including the actual allocated amounts. Existing request/report sizes, observation-work ceilings, computation lease, deadlines, cancellation and no-replacement rules remain in force. Historical v1/v2 reports remain inspectable without reexecution under a changed scientific build.

## Authored evidence

With 35 £20 deposits and flat 100 USD raw prices at 0.8 GBP/USD, the first momentum purchase owns 0.25 shares and equal weight owns 0.125 shares. An eight-USD dividend with two USD withheld per share and a £0.10 fixed fee produces £1.10 and £0.50 respectively: ending wealth is £701.10, £700.50 and £700 cash. Deliberately scaling the fee by shares fails this regression; exact sources were restored and the check passes.

Terminal cases preserve £700 under equal-value cash or 1:1 stock delivery. A confirmed zero in the final development month leaves momentum at £0, equal weight at £350 and cash at £700. Positive cash exit early in development funds subsequent periods without retaining the retired shares. A dividend after stock delivery includes all successor shares and produces £756 for both invested accounts in its authored fixture. Fully withheld income uses no FX; fixed fees require existing cash.

Regressions cover malformed policies at atomic admission, missing/wrong catalogs, terminal/delivery timing, unpaid/unsettled mark refusal, successor ownership, exact fees, source-bound replay and report recovery. These fixtures are authored engineering checks, including explicitly labelled false-provenance guard fixtures; they are not historical strategy results.
