# Explicit dividend entitlement and payment reference

The offline `RawGbpLedger` now separates a declared dividend entitlement from an explicit net payment. This extends the [raw GBP reference](raw-gbp-ledger.md); it has no application route, broker transport, automatic collection, study-selection or backtest integration. The dataset schema and existing notional reports are unchanged. Initial-v1 accounting acceptance remains partial.

## Cutoff, quantity and availability

`CaptureDividend` requires an available, effective, pure positive dividend action plus separate `RawDividendEvidence`: an explicitly asserted entitlement cutoff, its availability, source reference and SHA-256. The asserted cutoff must equal the action's effective instant. That assertion is a scenario/evidence requirement, not an automatic conclusion from a vendor field. The implementation does not establish legal record/ex-date entitlement, settlement rules, actual ownership or completeness of source history. Those facts need independently accepted source evidence before genuine use.

The current position must predate the cutoff. Intervening fills or later effective splits prevent retroactive inference of the earlier quantity. Earlier unresolved actions must be handled first; ambiguous simultaneous actions remain refused. Capturing entitlement freezes quantity, currency and `quantity × cash-per-share` in major currency units without changing GBP cash, contributions, fees or shares. Pence price units never scale a cash distribution. Selling shares or a subsequent split cannot sell or multiply the already frozen claim.

Unpaid income cannot fund purchases. `Value` refuses `raw-ledger-dividend-unpaid` while any claim is pending, including after selling all shares. It does not omit a receivable from purported total equity or invent its after-tax/FX value. The subsequent [explicit receivable valuation method](raw-dividend-valuation.md) can include pending income under separately declared assumptions; ordinary `Value` retains this settled-only refusal.

## Payment and conversion

`PayDividend` requires the original claim and a separate `RawDividendPayment`: a unique source payment ID, source reference/hash, actual paid time, availability time, original-currency gross and withholding, GBP fee and exact executable FX observation ID when money is converted. These are supplied accounting assumptions/source bindings. Hash shape validation does not read or authenticate a statement; retained artifact verification and broker mapping remain separate integration work.

Paid time cannot precede entitlement or exceed booking time; availability must be between paid and booking time. Scheduled announcements cannot stand in for actual payments. A later-observed payment is booked when known, with its original paid time retained; earlier cash/decisions are not rewritten. Gross and currency must exactly match the frozen claim. Withholding must lie between zero and gross, and fees must be non-negative. Partial/aggregated distributions, amount rounding, corrections and withholding refunds need a separate contract and remain unsupported.

For foreign net cash, the exact available FX observation must have the declared cash currency as base, GBP as quote and `Executable` purpose. Its observation time must be no later than payment and within the explicit maximum age **at payment**, rather than at later booking. A newer booking-date rate, inverted/reference rate, missing original ID or future observation is refused. GBP requires no FX observation. Fully withheld income has no conversion and carries no FX rate/assertion.

Cash changes by `(grossLocal − withheldLocal) × GBP-per-local-unit − feeGBP`. The fee alone increments the existing GBP fee counter; withholding remains separately recorded in local units. No tax advice, ISA eligibility or fiscal conversion policy is implied. Gross, withheld, fee, pinned FX, source and date details remain on the immutable payment receipt. Contribution totals and share quantities are unchanged. Source payment IDs must be unique within this ledger; one payment cannot be relabelled across claims. IDs should be namespaced by their supplying account/source.

Refusals leave event IDs, source payment IDs, cash, fees and claims unchanged. Duplicate capture/payment, borrowing, mixed action terms and changed/missing booked action evidence fail closed. Original receipts remain intact; identical later economic vintages remain usable under the existing binding check. The ledger remains in-memory and single-owner; durable recovery/reconciliation is not implemented here.

## Authored validation

Thirty-seven added cases bring the full .NET suite to 678 tests. The initial workflow test failed on the earlier refusal path. A deliberate substitution of gross for net cash makes its withholding assertion fail and is restored before validation. Tests cover paid versus available dates, pinned payment-time FX, unchanged cash before payment, post-cutoff trades, later sales/splits, prior split ordering, fully withheld income, GBP/pence amounts, mixed consideration, duplicate source payments, revisions after closing and rejected-payment atomicity/retry.

The central authored calculation deposits 300 GBP, buys two 100 USD shares at 0.8 GBP/USD, freezes four USD of dividend income, then sells one share for 80 GBP. A later payment withholding one USD, converted at 0.75 GBP/USD with a 0.2 GBP fee, adds 2.05 GBP: cash 222.05 GBP, one share worth 75 GBP and equity 297.05 GBP. A late booking must still use that pinned 0.75 conversion even if newer available FX is 0.9. Separate scenarios cover GBP dividends on pence-quoted shares and fully withheld income without an FX assertion. Four exported C# scenarios match independently recomputed Python Decimal cash, withholding, fees and frozen quantities; private receipts are under `/mnt/apps/developer/jellman86/artifacts/twenty/raw-dividends-20261021`. All financial inputs are authored; no genuine final window, production trial, vendor/broker request or accelerator is used.
