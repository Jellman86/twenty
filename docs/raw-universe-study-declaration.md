## Execution and selection conventions

Base policy freezes raw-price and FX maximum ages (1–92 days), nonnegative GBP reserve and fixed GBP fee per fill, and the existing fill policy: spread, slippage, local-major-currency order minimum, gross session-volume participation cap and quantity step. Stress doubles spread and slippage only. Fixed fees and every other constraint remain unchanged. A base policy whose doubled stress exceeds supported fill bounds refuses at declaration; values are never clipped.

The execution convention is the first reviewed opening strictly after the decision and no later than the next decision. Existing decision-pinned executable-purpose FX and same-open sale-funding assumptions remain model conventions. No additional FX markup, tax, settlement-cycle or cancellation/partial-execution recovery model is implied. Reviewed calendar/source payloads and actual broker costs remain separate acceptance requirements.

The frozen selection convention maximizes the weaker base/stress ending-GBP margin over the better same-universe equal-weight/cash account, with ordinal candidate tie-breaking. Imported research requires a positive worst margin or no candidate; engineering declarations serve lifecycle rehearsal only. Final intent is one explicit permanent spent claim, two final trials, same-request replay and no reranking. These rule identifiers declare the future workflow; this constructor implements neither score calculation nor admission/spending state. No investment nomination is granted.
