# Authored raw paper batches

Status: implemented first scope, undergoing acceptance. This is an independent persistent raw-share/GBP paper ledger, rather than playback of a study report or the existing model simulator. It deliberately supports authored inputs and complete offline batches only. Corporate actions, pending claims, asynchronous/partial fills, genuine forward evaluation and broker execution remain outside this scope. The initial-v1 paper gate remains partial.

## Frozen account and independent capital

`POST /api/research/raw-paper-accounts` binds a current authored study with a frozen development selection, its exact plan/selection hashes, one selected candidate and an explicit base/stress scenario. It reads the selection metadata and development bindings, never a final report. An account starts empty; no development/final balance or receipt is transferred. Only one account per study/scenario can ever be created, including after stopping or failure. Creation retries use the original UUID and return the retained account's current metadata without reading originals or reopening selection.

Creation supplies a separate authored dataset SHA, reviewed calendar pins, and a complete grid of 14–133 UTC dates. The first 13 dates establish signal history; the thirteenth is the first deposit/decision. The full grid must follow the source study's reserved end. Candidate, monthly deposit, execution costs, price/FX freshness, calendars, exact historical signal dates and future decision/end dates stay frozen. This prevents an operator choosing different within-month cutoffs after seeing outcomes. Dates remain consecutive UTC calendar months with inclusive 20–45-day gaps. The dataset manifest and calendar coverage must reach the grid end.

Explicit imports record a hash-checked provenance assertion in SQLite. Paper admission refuses genuine-flagged or unattested datasets from that metadata before opening market originals; legacy files need an explicit exact reimport, with no automatic startup scan/backfill. False synthetic flags and any corporate-action observation refuse before creating an account. The authored flag is not source authentication. Dataset bytes and reviewed calendar originals are retained privately and charged to the shared artifact quota. No final claim, research candidate, broker instruction or automatic purchase is created. The existing model paper accounts keep their own simulator and stored semantics.

## Applying a batch

`POST /api/research/raw-paper-accounts/{id}/advance` accepts `{requestId, expectedReceiptSha256, replaySha256}`. The cumulative canonical `RawReplayDocument` must already exist as `imports/<sha>.raw-paper.json`; the HTTP API accepts a hash, not a file path/upload. `expectedReceiptSha256` is null for the first period and the exact accepted receipt hash thereafter.

Each request preserves every previously accepted event exactly and appends one deposit, an explicit empty-claim opening mark, the frozen strategy-bound decision, every ordered modelled fill and a closing mark. The exact account grid fixes the signal history, decision and closing instants. No assumed fill, study binding, action, partial batch, extra deposit, changed price/FX convention or changed policy is accepted. A complete batch uses one reviewed calendar and one booking instant. No batch completion is inferred after a cancellation or partial fill.

The CPU archive independently reconstructs allocation and raw quantity intentions and replays the whole cumulative ledger from empty using the pinned authored dataset. It verifies fills, explicit executable FX, fees, cash and marks. Thus published account quantities and balances come from newly checked operations, rather than submitted balances. Exact decimal accounting stays in receipts; UI quantities/currency are display values.

An admitted attempt is journalled before this calculation. Its immutable request and receipt are retained under `raw-paper-operations/`. The successful operation and new account head/balance commit in one SQLite transaction. An original UUID returns its recorded operation even after later periods, a scientific-code change, stopping, or missing originals; changed payloads under that UUID refuse. A stale expected head refuses before a new attempt. New calculations require the original scientific/Core/runtime identity.

## Failure, restart and resources

Account states are `ready`, `active`, `running`, `completed`, `failed`, and `stopped`. The final declared batch completes the account. A retained failed/interrupted attempt stops further batches; another UUID cannot replace it or reset the account. Pre-admission worker/quota/head conflicts create no calculation attempt. `POST .../{id}/stop` stops future batches while preserving accepted results and all attempts; it is separate from the source study lifecycle.

At process restart, an uncommitted running attempt becomes interrupted and its account fails while retaining the prior accepted head, cash, quantities and contributions. A verified receipt orphaned before registry commit grants no accounting authority and is not automatically adopted. This first scope has no paper publication-recovery protocol. Cancelling a calculation retains an interrupted attempt; the whole offline batch is accepted or refused. These conservative limits must not be confused with the separate durable raw-final publication protocol.

There are at most 20 accounts, 120 admitted attempts per account, 10,000 cumulative replay events, 2 MiB canonical request bytes and 8 MiB receipt bytes. Existing archive observation/slice/work limits apply. The shared computation lease and two-minute cancellation deadline protect calculation. The store gate serialises mutations during a batch; there is no asynchronous order worker. An 11 MiB artifact-space preflight covers the maximum request/receipt plus bounded bookkeeping, and existing final-publication reservations remain protected. Foreign writes/physical storage exhaustion and fully coherent operator replacement of records are not prevented by artifact hashes.

`GET /api/research/raw-paper-accounts` discloses the authored-only/no-actions/no-asynchronous-fill boundary. `GET .../{id}/operations` reads the retained journal. `GET /api/research/raw-paper-operations/{id}/receipt` explicitly returns an accepted immutable SHA-bound receipt without market replay; merely opening an account does not load receipts. Writes use the host's authentication, same-origin/header policy and 16 KiB HTTP limit.

## Authored validation

Focused tests cover exact earlier-event preservation, fixed dates/configuration/costs/deposits, complete fills/marks, two-currency shares and fees, UUID aliases/conflicts, stale heads, retained terminal failures, unique accounts, worker/quota refusal, stopping/completion and restart after a publication transaction fault. In the hand-calculated example, £20 with a £0.25 fee buys 0.246875 shares at USD 100 and GBP 0.80 per USD, giving £19.75 marked equity and zero cash. Two batches give £40 contributions, £0.50 fees, 0.49375 shares and £39.50 marked equity. These values are authored accounting examples, not market performance.

Native API/browser, actual killed-process, compatibility and full-suite acceptance are recorded only after execution. Big Pickle's requested design review was blocked by its provider's free usage limit before inspection; no independent review verdict is claimed. The broader [raw-paper design](raw-paper-account-design.md), genuine-data/coverage gates, independent restore and combined initial-v1 acceptance remain open.
