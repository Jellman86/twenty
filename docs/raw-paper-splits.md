# Authored pure splits in paper batches

Contract inventory, 28 October 2026, baseline `d4968ae`: the backend now supports an explicit opt-in pure-split account. Dividends, terminal outcomes, pending claims and partial/asynchronous fills remain unsupported. UI integration belongs to the owner's other agent; [handoff contract](raw-paper-ui-handoff.md). This backend is not yet deployed; the [preceding preparation deployment](raw-paper-preparation-deployment-2026-10-28.md) is separate.

Creation freezes `actionMode: "pure-splits-v1"` and account version `raw-paper-split-batches-v1`. Omitted/null mode retains the existing no-action account, hash/JSON shape and manual replay contract. Core/archive/engine versions and decimal accounting stay unchanged. Research identity changes; older accounts remain inspectable, with exact UUID retries, but cannot silently continue under new scientific code.

Every action in the opt-in dataset must be a pure positive-ratio split, with at most 1,000 actions. Mixed consideration, symbol changes, dividends, terminal outcomes and revised/duplicate action vintages refuse admission. Equal effective or booking instants also refuse; this scope does not invent an order for overlapping actions. The immutable dataset binds the full observed split terms and availability; no settlement or claim policy is invented. Split booking is `max(effective, available)`. Ownership before the effective cutoff governs whether it applies. A late disclosure after quantity-changing history refuses through the existing CPU ledger guard. Any split intersecting a pending complete fill batch refuses, even if previously unheld, rather than reinterpreting its quantities.

Preparation processes every applicable split through the period close, including an exact closing-boundary booking once. The accepted event prefix stays exact. Application independently regenerates the canonical next batch from its frozen policy and originals, so omission or alteration of a split cannot be hidden by a coherent replay. No historical/source-study/final balance or report supplies capital.

Split targets and closing valuations additionally require a raw close at/after the latest known effective split for the relevant listing. The new paper seam refuses `raw-paper-split-price` when a share-count adjustment would otherwise be multiplied by an old-unit quote. This was a real cross-component regression: the missing refusal test failed before the guard. The generic standalone Core valuation API has not changed; do not infer this paper-policy guard applies to every raw-study or offline caller. Broader ledger quote-unit handling needs its own checkpoint.

TDD covers default-mode refusal; explicit HTTP opt-in; two-currency 2:1 share/price adjustment with unchanged equity; next-month quantities and exact restart retry; omitted event refusal; exact-boundary single booking; not-held-before-cutoff skip; unsupported/revised action admission refusal; split during a fill batch, old-unit raw quote and late quantity history refusal. The initial two unit/HTTP cases failed under the original action gate; 47 focused paper cases subsequently passed in development. Final source-bound execution counts are retained in `tests/tests.json` and `verified/verification.json` under `/mnt/apps/developer/jellman86/artifacts/twenty/paper-splits-20261028`; the full-suite floor is 1,433. All financial inputs are authored. Browser, genuine-source and broader action/claim acceptance remain separate. Mac testing is outside the owner's scope.
