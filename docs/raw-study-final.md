# One-time registered raw final evaluation

After [development selection](raw-study-selection.md), `POST /api/research/raw-studies/{id}/evaluate-final` accepts `{ requestId, planSha256 }`. It requires the frozen candidate, original selection bindings and current scientific/Core/runtime identity. Admission reads retained metadata, not original market values. One SQLite transaction permanently spends the shared holdout claim, records its exact UTC window, inserts only that candidate's base/stress requests and moves the parent to `final-evaluation` before either calculation can start. An artifact-write failure rolls the transaction back; uncommitted orphan files grant no authority.

The final request records the deployment commit that spent the claim; older stored records without that optional field remain inspectable. The original request UUID returns its recorded state after restart or code changes, including equivalent uppercase/lowercase UUID spelling. Another UUID refuses with `study-holdout-spent`, including after cancellation, failed execution or restart interruption. Failure stops the remaining pair without replacement. The claim and attempted trials remain visible; no runner-up, reranking or new final budget is created. Two unspent reserved slots become two registered attempts, so the shared lifetime budget is unchanged by the transition. Active final work remains reserved; the shared claim prevents that window being presented as unseen in a later study across engine/dataset aliases. Missing or inconsistent claim/window records fail closed.

`RawStudyFinalRun` (`raw-study-final-v1`) reuses the raw CPU calculation, calendar binding, ordered fill policy, action allocation and independent archive reconstruction. It retains the original plan/catalog/dataset hashes and final trial role. Final and development bindings cannot substitute for each other. The pure offline calculator grants no registration or claim authority; only the service's committed pair may enter its worker.

Every momentum/equal-weight/cash final account starts empty. A 73-decision plan receives exactly 24 reserved £20 deposits, £480 total; development cash, holdings, fees, entitlements and contributions never transfer. Final warm-up prices supply signals only, with no deposits or ledger ownership. The first allocation occurs at the reserved start and the last mark at the reserved end. Base and stress retain their original rules. Only actions effective and available in this stage are scheduled; a final action never enters development. Pending/unsettled claims at monthly marks still refuse, and mixed consideration remains unsupported.

Both verified reports must succeed. The final screen is the smaller base/stress momentum ending-GBP margin over each scenario's better equal-weight/cash account, strictly greater than zero to pass. It stores both report hashes and the worst margin. Synthetic studies always end as `engineering-complete`, even if the arithmetic screen passes; genuine-provenance studies record `research-complete` or `research-rejected`. This is the already-declared point-estimate convention, not an uncertainty adjustment, data audit, investment nomination or paper/broker permission. The candidate and original development selection remain unchanged.

History advertises `finalEvaluationAvailable: true`. Writes retain the same-origin/header guard and host body cap. There is no graphical raw-workflow authoring, raw paper promotion or automatic/live purchase path. Genuine-data coverage, pending-claim valuation across decisions, raw-family diagnostics and operational acceptance remain separate gates. Acceptance uses authored engineering fixtures; no genuine final window is opened to validate this plumbing.


## Failure and information boundaries

Final spending is deliberately permanent for every exposed attempt, including catalog/data refusal, deadline/shutdown, artifact quota/write failure or failed publication. A verified calculation that cannot be durably published is recorded as failed; clearing storage afterwards does not authorise another calculation or substitute report. The service does not implement a durable verified-result staging/republication protocol. Source/action completeness and storage/recovery readiness must therefore be assessed before requesting a genuine final evaluation. Missing final-window pins are checked after spending, so an unsupported action can consume the claim without yielding a report. Reading final action/terminal outcomes ahead of that claim would itself cross the information boundary; this workflow does not add a hidden preflight of reserved outcomes. Sparse quota-failure fixtures verify permanent exposure and the `final-failed` event after publication refusal.

The spent claim and its exact UTC window cover reserved **outcomes**, from `ReservedStart` to `ReservedEnd`. The trial's separate information footprint includes twelve historical signal decisions and the bounded stale-price lookback before those decisions. Already-known warm-up observations may overlap earlier research; they do not become new unseen final outcomes. Admission checks prior exposure against the reserved outcomes and active reservations against the complete information footprint. A backward clock at final admission refuses before spending instead of persisting a parent older than its selection; broader host-clock stability remains an operational requirement. Uncommitted artifact orphans may consume quota and require reviewed recovery; they never grant an admitted attempt.
