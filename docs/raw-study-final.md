## Failure and information boundaries

Final spending is deliberately permanent for every exposed attempt, including catalog/data refusal, deadline/shutdown, artifact quota/write failure or failed publication. A verified calculation that cannot be durably published is recorded as failed; clearing storage afterwards does not authorise another calculation or substitute report. The service does not implement a durable verified-result staging/republication protocol. Source/action completeness and storage/recovery readiness must therefore be assessed before requesting a genuine final evaluation. Missing final-window pins are checked after spending, so an unsupported action can consume the claim without yielding a report. Reading final action/terminal outcomes ahead of that claim would itself cross the information boundary; this workflow does not add a hidden preflight of reserved outcomes. Sparse quota-failure fixtures verify permanent exposure and the `final-failed` event after publication refusal.

The spent claim and its exact UTC window cover reserved **outcomes**, from `ReservedStart` to `ReservedEnd`. The trial's separate information footprint includes twelve historical signal decisions and the bounded stale-price lookback before those decisions. Already-known warm-up observations may overlap earlier research; they do not become new unseen final outcomes. Admission checks prior exposure against the reserved outcomes and active reservations against the complete information footprint. A backward clock at final admission refuses before spending instead of persisting a parent older than its selection; broader host-clock stability remains an operational requirement. Uncommitted artifact orphans may consume quota and require reviewed recovery; they never grant an admitted attempt.
