# Registered raw development worker

The raw-study API now supports an explicit development start after [atomic admission](raw-study-admission.md). Admission alone does not execute anything. This is a bounded research calculation, not final evaluation, a selected investment strategy or broker execution. The initial-v1 accounting, combined acceptance and genuine-data gates remain partial.

## Execution contract

`POST /api/research/raw-studies/{id}/start` accepts `{ requestId, planSha256 }`. The UUID identifies one irreversible start request for the exact admitted plan. An identical retry returns the retained family, including after completion, cancellation, restart or removal of the import inbox. Another start UUID cannot replace an attempted family. New starts and queued execution require the original scientific, Core and runtime identities to match the running build; historical inspection does not reinterpret old results under new code.

The worker records `started` before loading original inputs or calculating results. It reads only the retained, hash-checked dataset, calendars, action catalog and trial request. All eight candidate/cost entries remain registered. Each trial calculates independent empty momentum, equal-weight and cash accounts with the frozen development deposits, reserve, freshness limits and fill policies. Decision-time slices determine signals, weights and share intentions; subsequent slices provide reviewed-session fills and marks. The final window receives no development funding or evaluation.

The supported scope now applies declared pure splits and explicitly refuses dividends, terminal outcomes and order batches spanning multiple calendars. It does not selectively remove affected securities or candidates. Missing raw bars, executable-purpose FX, rejected orders, insufficient cash or any incomplete bound fill batch fail the attempted trial and stop its remaining family. Later final-window action rows do not gate development. Source-wide action discovery and dividend/terminal study scheduling remain required work.

Orders use the first supplied reviewed open after the decision, bounded by the next decision. Each batch is booked once the session closes and all selected raw bars are available; replay requires this same booking time. Sell-first funding, fractional quantities, historical FX labels and volume screens remain model assumptions. Calendar tables are stored once per replay request and referenced by their semantic hash; existing inline-calendar replay requests remain supported. Existing archive size and work limits are unchanged.

## Pure-split integration (26 October 2026)

The `raw-study-development-v2` receipt binds the complete original action-catalog bytes and a disposition for each split known and effective within the development window. Every such split in the supplied validated dataset requires a matching catalog pin, including actions on names an account never held. Missing or wrongly pinned originals, compound consideration, reserved event IDs and ambiguous same-time priorities are refused. Future final-window actions are excluded from development scheduling. This establishes coverage of the supplied file, not source-wide discovery or genuine-data certification.

The existing offline scheduler derives application time from the later of legal effectiveness and evidence availability and verifies the selected original vintage. At a shared timestamp, due actions precede a monthly mark and the next deposit/decision. A split affecting owned quantities during an outstanding fill batch is refused; orders are not resized or retrospectively repriced. Existing ledger checks also refuse disclosure after intervening quantity movements.

Ownership is reconstructed strictly before the cutoff from modelled opening times and prior split effectiveness, rather than later receipt times. Owned shares use the unchanged decimal split reference. An account with zero shares before the cutoff records `not-held-before-cutoff` and receives no split entry. A late disclosure after an owned position was sold cannot be classified solely from its current absence: historical exposure still requires accounting acceptance. Recovery rebuilds these dispositions and refuses omitted applications or forged skips.

This step deliberately retains refusal of dividends and terminal cash/stock outcomes. The existing offline catalog fixes absolute payment/settlement amounts for one declared exposure. Registered strategies hold different quantities, so per-share withholding, allocation/rounding, fees and settlement policies need an explicit frozen contract before those instructions can be applied across accounts. No proportional payment or zero deduction is inferred here. Existing standalone dividend/terminal accounting remains available with its original explicit evidence contract.

Authored 2:1 and 1:2 scenarios conserve £700 per account after 35 £20 deposits. In the equal-weight 2:1 case, 0.125 shares gain 0.125 shares; in the reverse case they lose 0.0625 shares, with matching raw prices preserving value. Tests cover exact catalog/vintage requirements, no-holding disposition, application before a shared mark/deposit, late-disclosure refusal, tampered applications/dispositions and retained-input worker publication. Report and original archive byte/work limits are unchanged; catalog lookup and ownership-history work are bounded. Reconstruction also charges deposits, decisions, historical signal slices, fills, actions and marks against the archive observation-work ceiling before allocation/replay. Historical reports remain inspectable without reexecution under the new scientific build.

## Lifecycle and integrity

Parent states are `registered`, `development`, `development-complete`, `failed` and `cancelled`. Child states retain planned, started, succeeded, failed, interrupted and cancelled attempts. A failed attempt is never replaced. A process restart interrupts a started attempt and stops its unstarted family; explicitly queued, unstarted work may resume. Cancellation preserves completed reports, requests and attempted rows, cooperatively stops a running calculation, and rechecks cancellation before publishing any report. An unresolved started row cannot be bypassed by starting another raw attempt.

The worker shares the existing computation lease and two-minute deadline. Queue order uses the explicit raw start time, rather than the earlier admission time, against model/universe admission timestamps. Active registered, developing and completed-but-unselected raw studies retain their shared reservation and two final slots. Cancellation/failure release unspent reservations but retain the original trial exposure and lifetime counts. No raw final claim is created.

`GET /api/research/raw-trials/{id}/report` returns an immutable SHA-bound report with three account summaries and original replay receipts. Before publication recovery reconstructs the expected request from the exact original plan, dataset, catalog and calendars. A fresh archive replay then independently checks strategy decisions, share intentions, ordered fills and marks and recomputes balances and summaries. Recovery also binds the report limitations. Historical report inspection checks retained hashes and metadata without reexecuting an old scientific build.

`GET /api/research/raw-studies` advertises `executionAvailable: true`, `finalEvaluationAvailable: false`. Writes retain the existing same-origin/header guard and 16 KiB host body limit. There is no raw final-evaluation endpoint, raw graphical authoring workflow, selection, paper-account integration, automatic purchase or live trading in this checkpoint.

## Verification

With a £0.25 fee, the single-asset momentum account pays £8.75 and ends at £691.25; two-asset equal weight pays £17.50 and ends at £682.50; cash remains £700. These are engineering examples, not historical performance.

Regressions cover full-window replay, changed deposits, altered limitations, missing/duplicate calendar tables, cancellation, preserved successful reports, missing retained inputs, interrupted-attempt restart, queue timing, explicit action refusal and byte-identical report recovery.
