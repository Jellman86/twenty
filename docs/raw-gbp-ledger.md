# Raw-quantity GBP accounting reference

`RawGbpLedger` is an offline decimal reference for explicitly supplied raw fills, positive GBP contributions and separately labelled mark-to-market values. It is not yet connected to changing-universe backtests, registered selection, paper accounts or broker submission. Existing notional reports and strategy decisions are unchanged. This is one dependency of initial-v1 gate V1-03, not its completion.

A fill declares a listing, signed share quantity, price in the known listing's raw quote units, explicit `Raw` basis and a non-negative GBP fee. A validated point-in-time slice supplies listing identity/units and the currency-to-GBP executable FX observation. The supplied price is an explicit fill assumption: a historical closing bar does not establish that a trade could execute at it. Quantity steps, minimum orders, spread, slippage and cost reserves belong to a later sizing/execution contract, now [proposed](execution-model.md) and not yet implemented; this ledger records a supplied quantity and fee without inventing those inputs.

For signed quantity q, raw quote price p, quote-unit scale u and explicit GBP-per-local-unit FX rate f, cash changes by `−q × (p × u) × f − feeGBP`. Pence has u=0.01, major units u=1. GBP needs no FX observation; foreign fills require the exact foreign→GBP direction and `Executable` purpose, observed/available by the fill time within the caller's explicit age bound. Rates are neither inverted nor inferred from reference series. Fees are GBP amounts supplied separately; FX spread already embedded in an actual conversion rate must not be charged again as an invented fee.

Valuation requires available raw bars within an explicit price-age bound and exact matching denomination. It reports quantity × raw major-unit close × selected GBP-per-local-unit FX plus GBP cash. The caller selects and the result retains the FX purpose. A reference-rate valuation is permitted as an explicitly labelled research mark, and cannot provide executable FX for a fill. Raw prices never fall back to split/total-return-adjusted bars. Publication/observation availability comes from validated slices; neither completeness of upstream history nor an independently executable quote is certified by those slices.

All admitted events have unique bounded identifiers and nondecreasing timestamps. Refused fills leave cash, holdings, fees and event identity unchanged; short positions, borrowing, missing FX, stale/future observations, adjusted fill basis and denomination reinterpretation are refused. Entire sales remove the quantity. State/receipt collections are immutable snapshots; this in-memory reference is for one owner, without a persistence/reconciliation promise. Event history is bounded to 10,000 entries. Receipts retain raw quantity/price/denomination, fee, cash movement and the selected FX identifier/rate. They do not replace a retained dataset/fill evidence archive.

Since the split-settlement checkpoint below, exact pure splits can be booked explicitly. The later [explicit dividend workflow](raw-dividend-accounting.md) separates entitlement and payment. Delistings, mergers and spinoffs affecting an open position remain unsupported. If such an effective action is known in a later slice, further fills and valuation refuse `raw-ledger-action-unsupported`. This prevents unadjusted old share quantities or omitted distributions from being treated as a complete total return. Pure symbol changes and neutral split/dividend revisions do not change quantities; additional economic terms cannot be hidden in those action kinds. Unknown/missing actions still require upstream coverage checks; absence from a slice is not proof that no action occurred. Settlement/payment dates and withholding need explicit evidence before implementing cash/stock events.

## Authored accounting checks

- Deposit 200 GBP; buy two USD shares at 100 USD with 0.8 GBP/USD and a 1 GBP fee: cash 39 GBP, two shares. At 110 USD and 0.75 GBP/USD, shares are worth 165 GBP and total equity is 204 GBP. Sell 0.5 share with a 0.5 GBP fee: cash 79.75 GBP, 1.5 shares worth 123.75 GBP, equity 203.5 GBP, cumulative fees 1.5 GBP.
- Deposit 25 GBP; buy two shares at 1,050 pence and pay 0.2 GBP: cash 3.8 GBP. Marking the two shares at 1,100 pence gives equity 25.8 GBP. Pence scaling never divides the share quantity by 100.
- Buy one USD share at 100 with executable FX 0.8 from 200 GBP. A later reference FX mark of 0.7 gives equity 190 GBP; requesting executable valuation from that reference-only slice is refused.

Thirteen new authored tests cover these values, unavailable/reversed/reference/stale FX, missing raw/stale prices, partial/full sale, read-only valuation, rejected-fill atomicity/retry, duplicate event identity, adjusted basis, short/borrow refusal, temporal/unit consistency and split refusal. The tests were written before the new implementation. A deliberate conversion-rate substitution to 1 also fails the foreign-quantity regression; it is restored before validation. An exported C# receipt also matches independently recomputed Python Decimal cash, partial-sale and FX/pence marks; private receipts are retained under `/mnt/apps/developer/jellman86/artifacts/twenty/raw-gbp-20261021`. All financial inputs are authored. No external quote, vendor/broker request, production account or genuine final evaluation is used.

## Explicit split settlement (21 October 2026)

`ApplySplit` resolves an available, effective `CorporateActionKind.Split` from the validated slice. Its positive new-shares/old-shares ratio multiplies a position held before the effective instant. Cash, contributions and fees do not change; non-monetary split receipts carry no FX rate or FX observation. The immutable receipt records action identity, original observation identity, effective time, ratio and quantity delta separately from booking time. This offline exact-quantity reference permits fractional results; it does not establish broker delivery precision, rounding or cash-in-lieu rules.

A late booking is allowed only without an intervening buy/sell in that listing at or after the action's effective time. Previously booked splits are compared by effective time, so successive late-booked pure splits can be applied in order. Earlier unsupported actions block settlement; simultaneous actions with ambiguous ordering remain refused. A post-effective buyer cannot apply an old split to newly purchased quantities. Duplicate action booking, missing holdings and future/unavailable actions do not consume event identity or mutate cash/quantities.

All booked action bindings remain checked, including after closing the position. Missing evidence or changed/withdrawn economic terms require reconstruction; no receipt or past fill is rewritten. A later capture with identical economic fields remains usable, preserving the original receipt's observation identity. Positive cash, successor, terminal or symbol-change terms accompanying a split are refused as complex rather than discarded. The ordinary holding guard also refuses mixed economic terms disguised as a neutral split, zero dividend or symbol change.

## Source-bound modelled fills (22 October 2026)

The [v3 execution path](execution-timing.md) can attach a verified plan/calendar to a fill. The ledger rechecks the exact raw session, source snapshots/hashes and pinned FX as of the modelled open, retains that evidence separately from booking time, and opens the holding at the modelled execution instant. Backdated receipts cannot spend later funds or omit an intervening economic action. Existing calls without this evidence retain the explicit supplied-price/booking-FX reference contract. This is offline accounting validation, with no broker route or actual-fill certification.
