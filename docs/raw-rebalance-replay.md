# Recomputed rebalance decisions in offline replay

25 October 2026. `raw-event-replay-v2` adds `RawReplayRebalanceDecision` to the existing bounded schedule/archive path. This closes the offline link between supplied weights, the reconstructed starting ledger and the resulting share intentions. It does not register a strategy or integrate raw execution into study selection, reports or paper accounts.

A decision event carries the complete original `RawRebalancePlan`. At that event's dataset/as-of instant, replay reruns `RawRebalancePlanner` against its own ledger, using the recorded universe, weights, deadline, fee, reserve and decision freshness limits. The complete serialized plan must match: valuation, quotes, source snapshots, FX pins, targets, ordered intentions, derived cash/fee amounts and limitations. Replay never injects a saved starting balance. Earlier deposits and assumed fills remain explicit caller assertions, retained in the archived request.

Every subsequent fill in a bound batch carries a `RawRebalanceOrderReference` naming the decision event and zero-based order index. Replay requires the exact next intention and fee. Each fill still independently passes the execution planner and ledger checks for price, calendar, FX, participation, cash and actions. Skipping, duplicating or reordering an order, removing its reference, substituting an order or fee, or inserting a deposit/action/mark before completing the batch refuses the entire replay. A no-op decision requires no fills and creates no fees. Separate later decisions may follow completed batches.

This deliberately accepts only uninterrupted, completely filled batches. It does not model cancellation, rejected-sale recovery, settlement cycles or a strategy that reacts to partial execution. Existing declared quantity-step rounding still applies; a successful modelled fill need not achieve the ideal target exactly. Fill policy and calendar remain explicit execution inputs, not parameters selected by the rebalance planner. The decision's freshness limits and the enclosing replay's execution/valuation limits remain separately declared.

The schedule compiler accepts decision events in its original timeline. Archive execution verifies them even when no schedule wrapper is present, and fresh verification repeats the same calculation. Removing both the decision and its order references produces a different, unbound request; local hashes are integrity checks, not signatures or proof that a caller supplied a registered strategy. Inspect the original request before asserting that a replay contains bound decisions.

The engine version changes to v2. Existing archives remain immutable and require their exact original Core/runtime and engine; they are not silently migrated or reinterpreted. No API, database, UI, broker, training or genuine evaluation is added.

Tests use the authored GBP 300 / two USD shares fixture from [rebalance planning](raw-rebalance-planner.md). Recomputed decisions preserve GBP 35.60 cash and 7.2 listing-B shares after the two modelled trades. Regressions alter original weights, starting cash/shares, quotes, derived fee budget and execution references; they also cover incomplete/duplicate batches, no-op decisions and verification without the schedule wrapper. These are synthetic accounting checks, not historical performance evidence.

Local acceptance passes 1,153 .NET tests, 45 Python tests, Ruff, Svelte checks (zero errors/warnings), the web build, research-boundary and sensitive-path/secret guards. A temporary plan-comparison bypass was caught by the input/evidence regressions before restoration and another full passing suite. A private completed-study clone retained its exact plan and ten request/report pairs; the original final UUID still replays while a replacement final request is refused. Proof is retained under `/mnt/apps/developer/jellman86/artifacts/twenty/raw-rebalance-binding-20261025` and `universe-study-cross-version-rebalance-binding-20261025`.
