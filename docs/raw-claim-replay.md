# Pending claims and explicit event replay

`RawGbpLedger.ValueWithReceivables` now marks pending terminal cash/stock consideration and unpaid dividends together. `RawEventReplay` runs an explicitly ordered sequence of deposits, fills, splits, entitlement captures, payments, deliveries and complete valuation marks into a new private ledger. Both are offline decimal references. They are not a registered raw backtest, automatic action scheduler or durable recovery format.

The later [verified offline archive](raw-replay-archive.md) now binds request/dataset bytes and re-executes a complete document during recovery. It does not add automatic scheduling or registered strategy integration.

## Complete estimated equity

Every unpaid dividend needs its existing `RawDividendMarkAssumption`. Every pending terminal claim needs one `RawTerminalMarkAssumption`, keyed by original listing/action. Missing, duplicate, null, unknown, settled or default assumption arrays are refused. Each terminal assumption states expected local withholding, expected GBP fee, availability time and source reference/hash; cash currency must match. Withholding lies within gross, fees are non-negative and evidence must be available at the valuation time. Stock marks require null cash currency and zero cash withholding. No tax, fee or cash-in-lieu default is inferred. Hash shape validation is a source assertion, not authentication.

Terminal cash is `(frozen gross − expected withholding) × current selected-purpose GBP rate − expected GBP fee`. It uses payout currency major units regardless of original quote units. Fully withheld claims need no FX, but an expected fee remains a liability and can make estimated equity negative without borrowing cash.

Stock consideration is marked as the frozen successor quantity at its fresh available **raw** price, using the successor's current denomination and valuation-time FX purpose. Pence quotes are converted to major units once. A separately held successor lot remains in holdings; only undelivered shares are valued in the claim. A missing/inactive/retired successor, missing/stale/adjusted-only price, inconsistent units or intervening economic successor action refuses the mark. Splits, distributions and further exits during the pending exchange window require reconstruction; adjusting an already-held lot does not resolve the pending lot automatically.

`RawGbpClaimValuation` reports holdings and cash, individual dividend/terminal claim marks, each claim subtotal and total estimated equity separately. Cash, share quantities, actual fees, contributions and ledger entries do not change. Matching actual settlement replaces its estimate once; settled claims reject stale assumptions. Earlier valuation receipts remain immutable. Existing `Value` stays settled-only, and `ValueWithDividendReceivables` continues to refuse pending terminal claims.

## Explicit chronological replay

Each `RawReplayItem` names an immutable market slice, unique bounded event identifier, zero-based contiguous sequence and typed operation. Booking timestamps must be nondecreasing. Sequence explicitly orders equal timestamps; replay never sorts input or invents priority between a payment and purchase. At most 10,000 items are accepted. A final complete mark is mandatory, so a pending or unhandled action cannot silently disappear from the returned portfolio.

`RawReplayAssumedFill` preserves caller-supplied raw price/quantity assumptions. `RawReplayModelledFill` additionally requires the original order, fill policy, calendar and fill receipt. Replay reruns the planner against the supplied slice and requires an identical sole fill with no rejection, then applies the ledger's independent session/source/FX checks. A changed receipt cannot substitute a cheaper price. A prior booked event **or valuation mark** later than the modelled opening time refuses delayed execution; later deposits or decisions cannot retroactively fund or ignore it. Planner refusal aborts this reference replay rather than returning a successful partial result. Assumed fills do not acquire the stronger modelled evidence contract.

All operations use the existing ledger's quantity, funding, action-history, physical-payment identity and evidence/revision checks. Failures return no completed replay and expose no partial ledger. Results contain immutable entries, marked snapshots, outstanding/settled claims, final holdings in ordinal identity order, cash, contributions and actual fees. Repeating identical supplied inputs yields the same serialized result. This does not establish capture provenance, cross-version durable serialization or historical completeness.

## Authored checks and remaining work

Examples start with 300 GBP, buy two 100 USD shares at 0.8 GBP/USD and leave 140 GBP cash. A 220 USD cash terminal claim with 20 USD expected withholding, 0.75 GBP/USD and a 0.5 GBP expected fee gives estimated equity 289.5 GBP; cash stays 140 GBP until actual settlement. Three successor shares marked at 80 USD with the same FX and fee instead give 319.5 GBP. An additional four-USD unpaid dividend, less one USD expected withholding and a 0.2 GBP fee, contributes 2.05 GBP at that rate. Cash-terminal plus dividend equity is therefore 291.55 GBP both before and after matching settlement/payment.

Regressions cover complete assumption coverage, both claim families together, stable identity ordering, separate existing successor holdings, pence/cross-currency terms, fee liabilities, future FX perturbation, unavailable/stale/adjusted prices, original action revisions and successor history. Replay checks tie ordering, late funding, altered modelled receipts, stock delivery followed by a split, bounds, malformed operations and incomplete final marks.
