## Frozen expectations and actual payment

Each existing dividend term may carry a separate `markPolicy` using `RawStudyClaimMarkPolicy`: action currency, expected withholding per cutoff share, one expected fixed GBP fee per entitled account, policy availability, source reference, content SHA and `raw-study-claim-mark-per-share-fixed-gbp-fee-v1`. A policy is required by the first unpaid monthly boundary, including the first decision, even if speculative ownership would be zero. Withholding cannot exceed gross and fees cannot be negative. A policy-free action paid before all marks is still valid. Source/hash validation binds supplied evidence; it does not authenticate a statement or tax assumption.

Capture/payment booking retains the original `max(effective, action available, cutoff proof available)` and `max(capture, paid, payment available)` rules. Every captured claim must settle by the account's last mark; an exact-final-boundary payment precedes that mark. Late proof cannot conceal a known held action: the shared ledger refuses valuation until the required action is booked. Equal cross-action effective/booking times, revised/mixed actions and events inside an indivisible fill batch retain their refusals. Pure splits, terminal proceeds, corrections, partial/asynchronous payments and combined actions are outside this mode.

Expected deductions remain separate from later actual withholding, fees and executable payment FX. Current mark FX values an unpaid claim; no future payment observation improves an earlier mark. The conversion from per-share assumptions to account totals uses the frozen entitled quantity once. No ownership produces no receivable, payment or account fee. At most eight actual claims can remain pending at a mark/decision; unheld catalog rows do not consume that limit. Metadata coverage checks remain conservative independently of actual ownership.

## Balances, funding and immutable operations

`balance.equityGbp` retains its historical meaning: holdings plus settled cash. Only the new mode adds `dividendClaims: {count, netExpectedValueGbp, expectedFeesGbp, expectedEquityGbp}`. The net receivable already includes estimated deductions. Actual `feesGbp` never includes an estimate. Stored summary shape/count/fee/total checks refuse inconsistent metadata without opening originals, including decimal overflow. A settled mark keeps a zero-count summary with expected equity equal to holdings/cash.

Opening/closing replay marks derive complete assumptions from the frozen policy and current unpaid entitlements. Claims plans target expected equity, while orders use delivered holdings, settled cash and permitted sales. Full receipts retain cash floors, reserve shortfalls, desired/funded quantities and deferred orders. Positive receivables cannot fund a purchase or cancel another claim's fee floor. Insufficient actual settlement cash refuses; preparation admits no attempt, and an admitted apply failure preserves earlier accepted capital with its immutable failed attempt.

Preparation reconstructs the accepted prefix and independently replays the generated archive. Application regenerates the exact canonical batch, so a coherent offline mark change cannot replace the frozen policy. UUID/head, computation lease, capacity, work, deadline, ordered-fill and one-account-per-study/cost controls remain. Stop retains the last accepted estimate; it does not settle a claim. Interrupted publication retains its attempt and orphan receipt without adopting an uncommitted deposit or payment.
