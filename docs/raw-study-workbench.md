# Raw-study workbench controls

The research page exposes the existing registered raw workflow: admit a prepared plan by SHA-256, start its eight development trials, freeze the recorded selection, explicitly request its one permanent final pair, stop work and inspect retained trials/reports. This is a control surface for the existing protocol, not graphical authoring of datasets, calendars or action policies. Originals must already be staged through the [inbox workflow](raw-study-admission.md).

Opening a study reads its retained metadata and trial register. Reports load only on an explicit report click, including final reports. Each development report exposes the raw-family diagnostic control with that trial as its anchor. Final reports do not expose a new diagnostic anchor. The report table shows independent momentum, equal-weight and cash accounts, actual booked fees, contributions and monthly marks. Current v4/v2 formats label expected interim equity and separate pending-claim estimates; older reports retain their original limitations and do not acquire invented claim counts.

Lifecycle writes bind the original plan hash and retain a request UUID across a failed response. Admission retains its UUID while name/hash are unchanged. New work is disabled when the plan's scientific identity differs from current capabilities; the server remains authoritative. Historical report/selection/plan links remain available. A late report response cannot replace a newer selected report or study.

Final spending is a separate explicit action, enabled only after acknowledging its permanence. There is no automatic final request after selection or polling. The claim remains spent after calculation, cancellation or publication failure, and missing final-window coverage can irreversibly fail the attempt. A completed screen grants no investment nomination, paper promotion or broker authority. These are the existing [final protocol](raw-study-final.md) rules, rather than new UI permission.
