# Cash dividends in authored paper accounts

29 October 2026. This adds `actionMode: "cash-dividends-v1"` and account version `raw-paper-dividend-batches-v1`. It is a bounded extension of the [paper-account contract](raw-paper-accounts.md), separate from the existing no-action and pure-split modes. It accepts authored positive cash dividends only, with every captured claim paid before each monthly mark. Combined splits/dividends, terminal proceeds, cross-month estimated claims, corrections, partial payments and asynchronous fills remain unsupported. No genuine entitlement, broker integration or investment result is established.

Creation freezes a `dividends` array alongside the original dataset, calendar pins, dates, strategy and cost policy. Each `RawPaperDividendTerms` binds a listing/action/observation ID, `RawDividendEvidence`, and `RawStudyDividendDelivery`. Every dataset action needs exactly one matching row; revised or duplicate action vintages refuse. The delivery retains the existing source-payment ID/reference/hash, actual paid/available dates, currency, gross/withheld amounts **per cutoff share**, fixed **per entitled account** GBP fee, exact payment FX observation ID, and `exact-decimal-per-cutoff-share-fixed-gbp-fee-v1` allocation rule. Its schedule point must bind the same dataset; replay event IDs and sequence are generated canonically by paper preparation, rather than taken from that point.

The dataset and asserted cutoff proof determine capture booking at `max(effective, action-available, proof-available)`. Payment books at `max(capture, paid, payment-available)`. If any monthly boundary, including the first decision, lies at/after capture but before payment, admission refuses `raw-paper-dividend-unsettled`, even if that account might not hold the listing. An exact-boundary payment precedes the closing mark and is never repeated at the next opening. Same-action capture/payment at one instant execute in dependency order. Shared effective times and cross-action booking ties refuse `raw-paper-dividend-ambiguous`; no market ordering is invented.

The shared CPU ledger freezes ownership before the cutoff. Without ownership there is no entitlement, payment or fixed fee. Intervening quantity history refuses under the existing ledger guard. Actual payment totals are derived once from the frozen quantity. Net local money is converted using its explicit, executable, payment-time FX pin; that rate can differ from the rate valuing shares or funding orders. A payment FX observation available at a monthly decision also affects ordinary valuation/sizing under the existing latest-available FX contract. It is not confined to the dividend. Contributions and share quantities never increase merely from capture; only settled payment cash can fund the following rebalance. A known fee still cannot borrow: an entirely withheld payment with no free cash refuses preparation as `raw-ledger-cash` without admitting an attempt.

Preparation reconstructs the accepted prefix, applies its own frozen action terms and executes an independent archive replay. Application regenerates the exact canonical batch and compares bytes, so changing withholding, omitting a capture/payment or substituting a different coherent offline result cannot replace the policy. Failed admitted applications retain their attempt and earlier accepted balance; preparations admit no attempt. Existing UUID/head checks, computation lease, request/receipt/storage/work limits and deadline remain in force. A corporate action inside an indivisible fill batch refuses, including an unheld action.

The API advertises `actionSupport.dividends: true`, `dividendMode: "cash-dividends-v1"`, `dividendScope: "paid-before-monthly-marks"`, and `pendingClaims: false`. Creation of this mode is API-only in this checkpoint; the existing graphical creation form continues to offer no-action and pure-split accounts. The Paper accounts screen recognises dividend accounts, labels them **Cash dividends**, and supports inspection, Prepare, explicit Apply and Stop without new dashboard panels. Full term authoring remains separate.

Absent `dividends` is omitted from historical JSON. No-action and split account versions/receipts retain their old shape, and a supplied catalog in either mode refuses rather than being ignored. Research assembly identity changes; pre-existing studies/accounts remain inspectable and exact operation retries work without originals, but new calculations under an older frozen identity refuse. Core accounting, engine/archive/report versions, study selection and holdout authority do not change.

Twenty added .NET cases cover arithmetic, payment boundaries, no ownership, catalog/refusal/retry/identity binding and an actual HTTP create → prepare → apply workflow. `scripts/raw_paper_dividend_workflow.py` additionally runs the native API and rendered Svelte at 1280/390px in a copied authored workspace, hides source reports during calculation, checks separate projection/application and cold retry without originals, and hashes source reports before/after. Its first run exposed the old UI's unsupported-mode gate before that adapter was updated. Private receipts are under `/mnt/apps/developer/jellman86/artifacts/twenty/raw-paper-dividends-20261029`; full and focused test counts are recorded by `checkpoint.py`, not inferred from filter names.

These are authored engineering checks. No genuine broker/vendor, final holdout, production paper operation, killed-process publication fault, independent peer review, or real dividend statement has been verified for this new mode. Publication/deployment status must be checked separately; implementation evidence alone does not claim it is live. The broader paper/data/initial-v1 gates remain open.

The subsequent [graphical dividend authoring checkpoint](raw-paper-dividend-authoring.md) adds creation through labelled fields, exact decimal request serialization and capability checks. It supersedes the API-only creation limitation above; the accounting scope and other evidence limits remain.
