## Confirmed findings and resolutions

- **P1 — corporate-action revision ancestry:** conversion reused the raw price bar's `Evidence.RevisionOf` for dividends and splits. A second capture containing an action therefore failed dataset validation with a cross-series ancestor; an action first appearing in a later capture could also name a price ancestor. Actions now maintain independent dividend/split revision chains. The regression pins the original amount, a revised amount and a cancellation in successive as-of slices.
- **P1 — withdrawn actions persisted:** a later `divCash = 0` or `splitFactor = 1` emitted no action revision, leaving an old action effective indefinitely. Neutral restatements now supersede the prior action without rewriting the earlier vintage.
- **P1 — stock consideration was liquidated implicitly:** `TerminalStock` passed through the cash-terminal cleanup, replacing successor shares with cash without a trade, sale costs or continuing exposure. The backtest now refuses stock-terminal panels with `universe-backtest-stock-settlement`. The panel may still report a simple stock valuation; carrying successor shares or explicitly modelling a costed sale remains required work.
- **P1 — incomplete stock-outcome checks:** a stock outcome with positive cash was accepted but the cash was ignored; successor splits/dividends and other corporate actions were not checked. Mixed consideration and self-successors now fail validation, and successor corporate actions require explicit settlement handling (`universe-panel-terminal-complex`).
- **P2 — incomplete vintage coverage checks:** only the newest capture's first session was checked. An intermediate capture could drop an interior or trailing session, leaving stale rows mixed with restated rows. Every successive vintage now retains the entire preceding session set; partial-window refreshes are refused pending a separate explicit merge policy.
- **P2 — same-instant capture handling:** conflicts were checked only at the newest instant, and identical retransmissions emitted tied revisions that failed validation. Conflicts are checked at every instant and identical retransmissions collapse into one vintage.
- **P2 — currency relabelling:** a validator-clean GBP identity could receive unchanged USD vendor prices under a GBP denomination. Conversion now enforces the USD-major scope of the supported identity contract.
- **P2 — enum serialization compatibility:** inserting `TerminalStock` before existing statuses shifted numeric `ExitedUnexplained`/`MissingPrice` values. Those retain their original values 4/5 and stock is appended as 6. Immutable outputs from the intervening local implementation require their original code/interpretation; they are not rewritten.
- **P2 — provenance and claims:** the history bundle hash omitted capture instants, and documentation implied genuine conversion evidence although the tests were authored fixtures. The bundle now includes source, UTC capture instant and payload hash. Documentation distinguishes the implemented path from a genuine conversion smoke, which remains outstanding.

Converter, panel and backtest versions advance to v2 to distinguish changed provenance and settlement semantics. The dataset schema remains v1; previously stored files are not migrated or overwritten.
