## Effects and limitations

Preparation freezes an explicit month-end UTC grid and all supplied execution fields. Hashes and provenance remain assertions until admission; preparation deliberately neither reads nor validates original inputs. Count/quota checks are under the shared store gate, include final-publication headroom, and preserve exact retries. Plan bytes have no new protocol version, random id or timestamp. No study reservation or final authority is created.

The UI uses editable example costs, requires review, displays the returned frozen policy/hash/dates, clears a preview after input changes, and copies the hash into a separate registration form. It does not author session/action source files or turn report inspection into paper integration. A local browser fixture intercepts writes; it must not be represented as a genuine-input or production experiment.
