## Responses in the implementation

- **Liquid-base churn and reserve refusal (H1): accepted.** The new planner uses expected equity for proportions, credits pending successor exposure and caps orders at settled cash/sale proceeds. Fully illiquid accounts disclose shortfall and defer purchases. The original settled-only v1 scope remains; the later implementation review additionally corrected its inherited fractional rounding refusal (below).
- **Per-share/total seam (H2): implemented explicitly.** One catalog-to-ledger helper multiplies withholding by frozen entitlement once; fixed fees remain per entitled account. Separate .25/.125/zero-share accounts pin the outcome.
- **Stage-end basis (H3): clarified.** The implementation refuses a pending stage end. It does not omit claims or switch to an understated successful ending mark. Opening/closing expected equity is consistent; a successful screen ends fully settled. This deliberately leaves unresolved stage-end claims unsupported.
- **Missing/late policy (H4): metadata preflight added.** Admission requires an available policy for every declared delivery interval crossing a monthly mark, including final-stage entries, before registering a family. Synchronous legacy entries can omit one. A general fee/tax policy may predate disclosure; it must be available at the first mark, without an invented requirement to postdate disclosure. Source-wide action discovery and runtime/settlement/publication failure remain separate limitations.
- **Opening denominator (M1): accepted.** An explicit opening mark follows each deposit and precedes the decision. Its equity is used directly, never with a second deposit addition.
- **Circular derived snapshot (M2): accepted.** Plans store declared assumptions; replay recomputes valuations and all derived intentions. Registered replay independently rebuilds assumptions from the original catalog. A coherent offline alternate estimate cannot replace that binding.
- **Compatibility (M3): bounded by existing identity contract.** New nullable/default fields preserve absent historical JSON. Final-v1/v2 inspection is allowed, with report/request version equality during current verification. Old unspent studies cannot resume under a changed Core identity. Exact historical inspection and UUID replay are distinct from scientific reexecution under another build; the latter is not promised.
- **Successor history (M4): conservative refusal retained.** No missing listing, price or pre-delivery distribution entitlement is invented. Existing successor listing/price/history guards remain; wider real-world merger support needs its own evidence contract.
- **Fees/liabilities (M5): cash floors and disclosure added.** Negative cash claims and stock delivery fees reserve real cash; estimates are separate from booked fees. Actual fee funding is still checked at settlement; a large liability can refuse a nonpositive opening. No future cash guarantee is claimed.
- **Work/size (M6): explicit bound added.** The new planner/study supports eight simultaneous claims. Per-claim observation work is charged even for cached marks; existing request/report and slice caps remain independently binding.

Reserved action point prefixes and refusal of legacy absolute-amount registered policies remain active. The fixtures are authored accounting checks, not genuine market, tax, settlement or profitability evidence. Implementation review and acceptance receipts will be added after completion.


## Implementation review and response

Both planners now reduce the buy quantity toward zero by adjacent representable decimal values until the exact ledger cash-change expression respects its floor. No arbitrary economic fee ceiling is invented. Two simultaneous maximum-fee policies exercise the mark overflow; fees are still independently declared expectations and cannot guarantee settlement funding.

Unpaid/unsettled stage-end codes remain ledger refusals. The legacy missing-policy regression is updated to the more precise code, and both stage-end paths remain tested.
