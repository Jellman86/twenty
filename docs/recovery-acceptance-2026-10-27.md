## Actual final-worker interruption

A separate fresh authored fixture at checkout `81f88b5` completed all eight development reports, froze its selection and queued one final pair with a synthetic claim. A native test process started that pair. After its row became `started`, the harness held a SQLite writer reservation only on this private workspace. Readers and calculation continued; the verified immutable final report appeared, but its registry publication could not commit. The harness then killed that process with SIGKILL and released its own reservation. This is an explicit test interleaving, not a production lock or a source-row rewrite.

The persisted row was still `started` without a report hash. Real application startup changed it to `interrupted` with `process-restarted`, cancelled its partner and left the study `final-failed`. The original final UUID replayed that outcome, a replacement UUID refused `study-holdout-spent`, and the orphan report could not be read through the successful-report endpoint. All eight development report hashes and ten original request hashes stayed exact; one synthetic and zero genuine claims remained. The local processes were stopped. Records are under `/mnt/apps/developer/jellman86/artifacts/twenty/active-final-recovery-20261027`.

This pins the declared publication-loss boundary with an actual killed process and a partially published artifact set. It does **not** implement publication recovery: the verified result remains lost to the registered screen, and the retained orphan still consumes storage. Storage-pressure, mounted restore and combined final acceptance remain open.
