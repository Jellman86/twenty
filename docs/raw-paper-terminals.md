## Scope and frozen terms

Each row binds a listing, action and observation to `RawTerminalEvidence` and an explicit nullable `RawStudyTerminalDelivery`. Cash and successor amounts are per retired cutoff share; the GBP fee is per entitled account. Actual currency, withholding, fee, settlement/availability times, source identity/hash, FX pin and allocation rule are explicit. Mandatory constructor fields cannot silently become zero. Confirmed zero requires `delivery: null`; cash/stock require a complete delivery. Stock carries no cash/withholding/FX fields. Mixed consideration, revisions, additional splits/dividends and repeated terminal actions for one listing refuse. The supplied catalog must cover every observed action; source-wide discovery remains separate.

Capture is booked at the latest of effectiveness, action availability and cutoff-proof availability. Settlement is booked at the latest of capture, actual settlement and settlement-evidence availability. Its executable FX is pinned at actual settlement, independently from the monthly market conversion. Hash validation binds supplied evidence; it does not authenticate the original or establish legal entitlement.

All monthly boundaries, including the first decision, must be settled. Exact-boundary settlement precedes the mark; a pending boundary refuses `raw-paper-terminal-unsettled`, even if ownership might later prove zero. This first mode carries no expected terminal valuation policy. Missing/late proof cannot conceal a known held action: ordinary ledger guards refuse valuation until the required action is booked. Equal cross-action effective/booking times refuse as ambiguous. Actions cannot interrupt an indivisible ordered fill batch.

## Ownership, cash and immutable operations

Capture removes the old position exactly once. A confirmed zero writes it off without cash or fees. Cash settlement multiplies gross and withholding by frozen entitled shares once, applies the actual executable FX and one fixed fee. Stock settlement delivers the frozen ratio times entitled shares; it neither creates cash nor invents a purchase. No cutoff ownership produces no claim, settlement or fee. The shared ledger retains denomination, quantity-history, successor-history, source/revision and borrowing guards. Explicit later retirement of delivered successor shares reconstructs their actual delivery-time ownership.

Settled cash funds later decisions. Pending cash/stock cannot fund purchases or sales in this mode because unpaid monthly boundaries are refused. Stock fees still need actual cash; fully withheld cash needs no FX and cannot borrow its fee. `equityGbp`, cash, contributions, actual fees and holdings keep their meanings. There is no terminal estimate or dividend-claim summary on these accounts.

Preparation rebuilds the accepted prefix from frozen originals and independently replays the generated archive. Application regenerates the exact canonical next batch. A coherent standalone archive with altered settlement terms cannot replace the account's frozen policy. UUID/head, one-account-per-study/cost, lease, work, capacity, deadline and interrupted-publication controls remain. Stop retains accepted capital; an exact retry needs no originals. Metadata inspection refuses a missing new catalog or one injected into an older mode.
