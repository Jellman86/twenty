# Price conversion evidence contract (`listing-prices-v4`)

Implemented 19 October 2026 on `dev`, advancing [#5](https://github.com/Jellman86/twenty/issues/5) and completing the step the [listing identity contract](listing-identity.md) explicitly left open: `Twenty.Ingestion.Listings.ListingPrices` converts captured Tiingo daily histories into [v1](data-contract-v1.md) `PriceBar` observations (raw and total-return-adjusted) plus split/dividend `CorporateAction` observations, over a validated two-source identity dataset. The result is a dataset shape that `ChangingUniversePanel` can price from forward-collected evidence. The converter has authored-fixture coverage and a bounded genuine AAPL conversion smoke, recorded below.

```csharp
var result = ListingPrices.Convert(store, identity.Dataset, dataset-id);
```


```sh
dotnet run --project src/Twenty.Data -c Release -- listing-prices \
  --dataset <identity.json> --archive <private-capture-copy> \
  --dataset-archive <private-output> --created-at <ISO8601-with-offset> \
  --dataset-id <id>
```

`--created-at` is explicit for reproducibility, must include an offset and cannot exceed the current clock. It must also cover all used captures under the dataset validator. Repeating the same inputs and cutoff produces the same immutable content address. Work from a private offline copy of the capture store, never open the running app's writer store with this command.

Inputs are a `CaptureStore` holding Tiingo history response captures and the `DatasetFile` produced by `ListingIdentity.Convert`. Output is `ListingPricesResult(Dataset, BarsAdded, ActionsAdded, OutsideIntervalSkipped, Excluded)`; raw captures and datasets stay outside Git.

## Request ticker identity (20 October 2026)

Legacy source buckets replaced dots with hyphens, so `AAA.B` and `AAA-B` shared `tiingo-history-aaa-b`. The bucket alone cannot identify a history. All history readers now require its recorded HTTPS `api.tiingo.com/tiingo/daily/<ticker>/prices` URL to identify the requested ticker and agree with its source bucket. Valid captures of the other ticker in a shared legacy bucket are ignored for this listing; missing/invalid URL identity fails closed (`tiingo-history-provenance`, or the terminal audit's existing request refusal). Missing target history remains an explicit exclusion, never a substituted price series.

New unpunctuated captures keep their original IDs, such as `tiingo-history-aapl`. Punctuated tickers use `tiingo-history-v2-` followed by lower-case ticker characters with every dot escaped as `-d` and every hyphen as `-h`. Thus `AAA.B` and `AAA-B` become `tiingo-history-v2-aaa-db` and `tiingo-history-v2-aaa-hb`. Encoding every hyphen prevents escape aliases and stays within the capture store's 64-character bound for the maximum 20-character ticker. Readers inspect both canonical and legacy buckets without duplicating an unpunctuated bucket. URL validation is still required because a new bucket can coincide with another ticker's legacy bucket.

Original capture logs and datasets are not migrated or repaired. The converter advances from v3 to v4 to record the stronger provenance requirement; the Python wire auditor advances to `listing-prices-wire-v3` and independently keys histories by their original URL ticker. Existing valid AAPL observations and the source-bound action/terminal reports are preserved in the [replay evidence](ticker-identity-smoke.json). This binds recorded metadata, not source authenticity or historical symbol-reuse identity; the validated listing interval remains required.

## The contract

- **Availability is capture time.** Every bar and action from one capture cites that capture's `ObservedAt` as both published and available, with `ObservedPublication` evidence. A vendor's current vintage becomes *effective* history — session dates back to whenever the symbol traded — but it can never be presented as point-in-time knowledge from before it was captured. `PointInTimeDataset.AsOf` therefore hides all prices before the capture instant, exactly as it hides identities (see the identity doc's availability consequence).
- **Vintages merge rather than replace.** Every capture that carried a session emits a bar (and any dividend/split action) for it, available from that capture, with later restatements linked through `Evidence.RevisionOf` on the `(listing, session, basis)` series key — the same key `PointInTimeDataset` dedupes by, so each decision sees the vintage that was actually knowable then. Same-instant captures at any vintage must agree on content (`price-conversion-capture-conflict`), and identical retransmissions are collapsed. Every later capture must retain every earlier session (`price-conversion-vintage-narrower`), including interior and trailing sessions; empty captures are also refused. Partial-window refreshes need a separate explicit merge policy.
- **Two bases per session**: `PriceBasis.Raw` from the raw OHLC fields and `PriceBasis.TotalReturnAdjusted` from `adjOpen/adjHigh/adjLow/adjClose`. Volume follows the same split: the raw bar carries raw share `volume` and the total-return bar carries `adjVolume`, so neither bar mixes an adjusted price with an unadjusted count. Bar ids are `<listingId>-raw|tra-<yyyymmdd>`. Session closes follow the bounded [Nasdaq cash-equity calendar](nasdaq-sessions.md): regular 16:00 and declared early 13:00 local, with DST-correct UTC instants. Weekends/holidays, unsupported years and other venues fail closed.
- **Adjustment consistency is a conversion-time exclusion.** Every adjusted field on a row must imply the same factor as `adjClose` (relative tolerance `1e-6`); one row describing two adjustments excludes that listing (`adjustment-inconsistent`). The parse layer only requires the adjusted fields to be present and valid (adjusted OHLC positive, `adjVolume` non-negative); genuinely inconsistent rows are dropped from the dataset rather than failing the whole conversion, and the row-level step checks remain the [audit](listing-audit.md)'s job.
- **Corporate actions** come from the vendor's own `divCash`/`splitFactor` on each row: `CorporateActionKind.Dividend` with `CashPerShare` and currency, `CorporateActionKind.Split` with `SplitRatio`, effective at the session close. Ids are `<listingId>-div|split-<yyyymmdd>`. These are vendor claims verified only for internal consistency against the adjustment steps; they are not reconciled against exchange notices.
- **Rows whose session close is after the capture are counted, not converted** (`InProgressSkipped`): an intraday capture carries the in-progress session, which is not a completed bar; conversion is only honest after the US close. Use `InProgressSkipped` to detect a capture taken mid-session.
- **Rows outside the proven active listing interval are counted, not converted** (`OutsideIntervalSkipped`): a bar trading after the identity-proven delisting instant is either stale vendor data or a symbol-reuse successor — never silently attached to the identity. A listing whose capture is absent (`history-not-captured`) or whose rows all fall outside its interval (`no-prices-in-interval`) is excluded, and its identity rows are dropped with it: an untrusted or unknowable price never reaches a priced dataset.
- **Action corrections** use separate dividend and split revision chains, never price-bar ancestors. A withdrawn dividend is revised to zero and a withdrawn split to ratio one, retaining the previously known evidence. The converter accepts USD-major identities only (`price-conversion-denomination`); it cannot relabel USD numbers as another currency.
- **The manifest** records the reviewed calendar version/hash for converted listings, replaces the `identity-only` feature with `daily-prices`, adds a `tiingo-history` source snapshot (bundle hash of the source, UTC capture instant and payload hash of each used vintage, retrieved at the latest of them), and sets `CreatedAt` to the later of the identity's and the latest used capture for deterministic ordering.
- **Determinism**: conversion is a pure function of the identity dataset and the store's captures; test-identical inputs serialize byte-identically.

## Why a genuine multi-year backtest still needs wall-clock time

Two constraints once stood between the converter and a genuine monthly panel; one is now closed in code.

1. **Vintage merging — implemented.** Every capture that carried a session emits its own bar (and any action) for it, available from that capture, with later restatements linked as `RevisionOf` chains on the `(listing, session, basis)` key, so accumulating captures genuinely build point-in-time price history rather than each new capture superseding the last. A later capture that drops any earlier session is refused (`price-conversion-vintage-narrower`), including a capture that carries no rows at all.
2. **Availability-at-capture — a wall-clock wait.** A session's price becomes knowable only at the capture that first carried it, and decisions may not exceed the dataset's `CreatedAt`, which must be at least the latest capture used. The backtest evaluates from its thirteenth period, so a genuine panel needs at least fourteen monthly decisions, all inside `[capture, CreatedAt]`; that span only grows with recurring monthly captures. No code change removes this — it is the price of not presenting a vendor's current vintage as retroactive knowledge.

The registered pipeline test stops where honesty stops: captures → identity → conversion → point-in-time panel, asserting prices appear exactly at capture and the first monthly decision prices cleanly. The panel builder's `UniverseBacktest`-level coverage continues to use authored synthetic fixtures, and the French panels remain fixed-universe research-only inputs.

## Validation

Tests in `tests/Twenty.Tests/ListingPricesTests.cs` cover: both bases at DST-correct session closes with point-in-time availability and manifest features, including raw `volume` on the raw bar and split-adjusted `adjVolume` on the total-return bar; dividend/split action emission and outside-interval counting; adjustment-inconsistent exclusion that keeps the rest of the dataset; the duplicate/source-conflict/capture-conflict/empty refusals; vintage merging with each bar available from its first capture and `RevisionOf` chains across restatements; a later capture that drops an earlier one's first session refused as `price-conversion-vintage-narrower` (as is a capture that carried no rows at all, which previously crashed); determinism; `ParseHistory` rejecting legacy rows without adjusted OHLC or `adjVolume`; and the captures → identity → prices → point-in-time panel pipeline over an authored varying-price fixture. All inputs are **authored synthetic fixtures** shaped like genuine Tiingo responses (including the adjusted fields the strict parser now requires); no genuine capture was converted in the test suite, and the genuine Tiingo payload schema (`adjOpen/adjHigh/adjLow/adjVolume` present on every row) is what makes the strict contract safe.

The 20 October review added regressions for action restatements and withdrawal, duplicate/contradictory same-instant captures, intermediate-vintage holes and currency relabelling. See [the review](reviews/2026-10-20-post-muse.md). Version 2 distinguishes these changes from previously generated artifacts.

## Genuine captured-history smoke (20 October 2026)

The existing reviewed identity snapshot `aab59f90…` and private copies of the already captured Tiingo responses produced dataset `39657542…`: 5,980 observations, comprising 2,965 raw and 2,965 adjusted AAPL bars plus 47 dividend and one split observation, with the listing and membership preserved. Sessions span 2015-01-02 through 2026-10-16. The full v1 validator passes, and identical repeated conversion produces identical bytes. No network collection or production-data import was required.

ABMD, COST and MSFT were excluded as `history-not-captured`. BBBYQ's existing history was not attached to AAPL or invented as a NASDAQ listing: its successor OTC identity was already excluded by the identity review. This smoke therefore establishes one active listing conversion, not genuine delisting accounting or whole-market coverage.

`scripts/audit_listing_prices.py` independently reads the archived producer JSON using Python decimal arithmetic, verifies payload hashes and compares every converted OHLC/volume field, action amount, session close and capture availability. It also checks that no expected bar/action was omitted. All 5,930 bars and 48 actions match. This is a same-producer wire check; the script deliberately mirrors the declared 16:00 close assumption and cannot certify holidays, early closes or economic correctness. Negative controls reject changed price/volume/time/action values and omitted observations.

The AAPL capture predates the directory evidence on 18 October. A price cannot be resolved through `DataSlice.Prices` until the corresponding identity is also knowable, even when the price observation itself was captured earlier. A September→October panel contains only an October entry and no historical return; `UniverseBacktest` refuses it with `universe-backtest-history`. No final evaluation or forecasting result was generated. Source hashes, exclusions, exact output hash and limitations are retained in [the smoke metadata](genuine-price-conversion-smoke.json); payloads and output datasets stay outside Git.
