# Recorded raw-family uncertainty

The diagnostic journal accepts a completed registered raw development trial as its anchor. `stationary-raw-active-mean-v1` applies the existing [joint stationary bootstrap and selection kernel](search-diagnostics.md) to the four upfront momentum candidates in that study under the anchor's single base or stress cost scenario. Each candidate uses its own recorded equal-weight and zero-interest cash comparators. The caller supplies an anchor, never a winner shortlist.

All four declared candidates must have succeeded with their retained original requests and report hashes. An incomplete, failed or cancelled member refuses the analysis (`raw-diagnostic-family-incomplete`); it is not dropped. Other studies and the opposite cost scenario remain disclosed with explicit exclusion reasons. Final trials are always excluded, and a final anchor refuses before reading its report. Model, changing-universe and raw registers and raw plan metadata are captured together under the research state lock. All local attempted trial counts enter only the kernel's explicitly labelled count-as-independent sensitivity, not an estimated independent sample size or whole-programme correction.

## Recorded inputs and basis

Raw monthly returns use expected opening/closing GBP equity, including separately declared pending-claim estimates. The deposit appears once in the opening denominator. Successful stage ends are fully settled. Cash for trading remains separate from estimates; this diagnostic does not alter funding or settlement. FX, deductions, successor availability and execution assumptions retain the original report's limitations. No external market benchmark is invented, and the changing-universe method's no-FX limitation does not describe raw GBP accounting.

The adapter reads hash-bound development requests, reports and their original receipts. It checks the snapshot's exact hashes again while loading, full ordered account roles, original plan/trial/dataset/build bindings, the complete contribution/decision/end grid, opening and closing mark summaries, recorded fee/contribution totals and period-return identity. It checks receipt hashes and structural consistency without reexecuting a ledger or reading a dataset original. The worker's original reconstruction/replay remains the accounting evidence; a diagnostic is not a new certification of its financial inputs. Missing or unreadable request/report files receive their respective integrity refusal codes. Corruption or a changed/disappeared row fails with the journaled complete snapshot retained. A deterministic test mutation after snapshot journaling confirms that the original hashes remain in the failed attempt and the changed row is refused.

New analyses require development-v4 report semantics; older eras remain inspectable but refuse new raw analysis (`raw-diagnostic-report-era`). Same-era reports from an older scientific build can be analysed as recorded evidence while their nested archive/replay formats remain supported, with their original hashes/build bindings retained. This grants no resumption of an old unspent study: start, selection and first final spending retain their current-science guards. An unsupported nested format also receives `raw-diagnostic-report-era`, rather than an integrity accusation. No final report, final return stream, final margin or market original is consumed by the diagnostic calculation.

## Journal, interface and bounds

The existing `{ requestId, anchorRunId }` endpoint, same-origin/header guard, one diagnostic worker, two-minute deadline, cancellation, interruption handling and journal byte/count limits remain. The family is exactly four candidates, with at least 24 complete monthly returns required by the kernel. The kernel also retains its monthly return range of −100% to 10^12. A ledger report with negative expected month-end equity can be valid under a declared pending-fee scenario but fall outside this diagnostic range; the whole family then refuses with `diagnostic-return-stream`, without clipping the return, dropping the candidate or changing the ledger report. An authored liability fixture pins this boundary. The usual 3/6/12-month block sensitivities and 2,000 shared resamples are unchanged. The authored 35-month development fixtures are insufficient for the existing 48-month selection-statistic gate, so their unavailable selection estimates remain explicit.

New raw attempts record `familyEngine: raw-universe`, original `rawPlanSha256`, `rawCostScenario` and required `rawReportVersion`. Optional metadata is omitted on historical entries; retries return the original journal outcome, including a pre-implementation failure. Successful and excluded raw rows retain their recorded request/report/scientific hashes. New model/universe attempts also retain raw rows’ report hashes and candidate/cost attribution; new model attempts record `familyEngine: model`. These additive disclosures do not rewrite historical attempts or change their retry bytes. Analysis adds no research trial, holdout claim, selection, paper promotion or broker authority.

The history listing identifies the engine, raw cost scenario and report era when recorded. Opened raw attempts also identify each candidate’s original trial id, top-name count and weight cap alongside the actual comparator roles. Earlier journal entries retain their missing metadata. A history entry from another anchor states which selected run a new analysis will use. Late history responses cannot replace a newer selection. Raw analysis is currently started through the API, then inspected through diagnostic history in the existing model/universe panels. There is no raw study graphical entry point yet. Graphical raw study authoring and broader paper integration remain separate roadmap gates.

Corrupt original requests, coherent report-grid/era rewrites and forged receipt hashes refuse with the full snapshot preserved. A deliberately damaged final artifact is never read when refusing a final anchor.
